# Privacy

Salah Companion collects nothing.

- **Camera:** frames are processed in memory on your phone to find body positions, then
  discarded. No photo or video is ever saved or sent anywhere.
- **No data leaves your phone.** The Android app requests no internet permission. The iOS
  app contains no networking code.
- **No account, analytics, crash reports, ads or tracking.**
- **What is stored:** only your settings (chosen surahs, Auto rotation position,
  opening-dua length, pace and volume), on your phone. Android auto-backup is turned off
  for the app.
- **Temporary audio cache:** to play the recitation, the audio player keeps temporary
  copies of the app's own bundled audio files in the app's cache folder. They are cleared
  every session (when the app starts and when each prayer session ends) and never contain
  any of your data.
- **Microphone:** not used.

Store declarations: Google Play Data safety "No data collected, no data shared"; Apple
App Privacy "Data Not Collected".

## Internal test app (developers only)

A separate internal test app, with its own package ID and never published to app stores,
can save body-point numbers (never images or video) from team members and volunteers who
explicitly agree, to tune detection. These files are kept by the project and never
combined with any personal information.

Found a privacy problem? Please report it privately through a GitHub security advisory
on this repository.
